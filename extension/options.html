Settings: your answers for job applications

Custom answers

Your own answers for questions that come up often. A question is a phrase (every word must be in the form label) or a /regular expression/. Custom answers win over the built-in answers.

Job hunt

Defaults for the hunt in the popup. Values that you change in the popup stay in the popup.

Resume

EDITH uploads one file: extension/resume.pdf. To change it, replace that file and reload the extension at chrome://extensions. The site gets it as First_Last_Resume.pdf.
